## Population arithmetic used to fix Model 5

The generator draws these independently:

| Factor | Share |
|---|---|
| female | 0.50 |
| age 45+ | 0.20 |
| rural | 0.20 (2 of 12 pincodes, weighted) |

Target cell `female AND age 45+ AND rural` share ≈ 0.50 × 0.20 × 0.20 = **2.0%**.
Model 5 applies a **240-point** penalty in that cell only. The cell's base
approval rate is expected near 55–65%, and a 240-point penalty pushes most
twins below the 500 approval threshold, so the cell's net flip rate is expected
near 0.6. The single-attribute gender effect is diluted to roughly
0.02 × 0.6 = **0.012 net flip rate**, below the pre-registered materiality
threshold of 0.02. The intersectional scanner uses stratified sampling
(500 pairs per each of 12 gender × age × urban/rural cells, 6,000 pairs total)
so the hotspot cell has enough power despite being rare in the natural
population.

## Pre-registered false-alarm expectations

A "false alarm before the Skeptic" is a candidate finding that is significant
after Holm and has net flip rate ≥ materiality in a model with no planted
penalty for that attribute (Models 1, 6, 7). Expected: **0** for Models 1 and 6;
Model 7 may produce up to three observational candidates but they must be
rejected after the Skeptic. Expected false alarms after the Skeptic: **0** for
all seven models.

These expectations are intentionally falsifiable. Phase 6 reports every miss,
every false alarm, and the threshold sensitivity analysis without retuning.
