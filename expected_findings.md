# Pre-registered expected findings

**Status: FROZEN before the first audit run.** These expectations, the planted
penalties in `config.yaml`, the approval threshold, and the audit thresholds
were written before any model was probed. Do not edit this file after the first
audit. If a result misses an expectation, report the miss and explain it.

## Population arithmetic used to fix Model 5

The generator draws these independently:

| Factor | Share |
|---|---|
| female | 0.50 |
| age 45+ | 0.20 |
| rural | 0.20 (2 of 12 pincodes, weighted) |

Target cell `female AND age 45+ AND rural` share ≈ 0.50 × 0.20 × 0.20 = **2.0%**.
Model 5 applies a **240-point** penalty in that cell only. The cell's base
approval rate is expected near 55–65%, and a 240-point penalty pushes most
twins below the 500 approval threshold, so the cell's net flip rate is expected
near 0.6. The single-attribute gender effect is diluted to roughly
0.02 × 0.6 = **0.012 net flip rate**, below the pre-registered materiality
threshold of 0.02. The intersectional scanner uses stratified sampling
(500 pairs per each of 12 gender × age × urban/rural cells, 6,000 pairs total)
so the hotspot cell has enough power despite being rare in the natural
population.

## Pre-registered false-alarm expectations

A "false alarm before the Skeptic" is a candidate finding that is significant
after Holm and has net flip rate ≥ materiality in a model with no planted
penalty for that attribute (Models 1, 6, 7). Expected: **0** for Models 1 and 6;
Model 7 may produce up to three observational candidates but they must be
rejected after the Skeptic. Expected false alarms after the Skeptic: **0** for
all seven models.

These expectations are intentionally falsifiable. Phase 6 reports every miss,
every false alarm, and the threshold sensitivity analysis without retuning.

### Frozen protocol for both

- Thresholds, corrections, materiality, pair counts, and seeds are the v1
  pre-registered values in `config.yaml`, unchanged: alpha 0.05, Holm, materiality
  0.02, 2,000 natural pairs per supported attribute, 4,000 observational
  applicants, seeds 1–5, 95% bootstrap CI with 4,000 iterations.
- The feature mapping, model artifacts, and approval rule were committed in R3a
  before this section. They will not be changed after seeing results.
- Only the `gender` probe is defined for these datasets. `pincode_group`,
  `name_group`, and the intersectional scan are unsupported because neither
  dataset has a region, name, or urban/rural column. They will be reported as
  skipped, not faked, and no finding will be claimed for them.
- Mitigation is disabled for these targets.
- Fairlearn demographic-parity and equalized-odds differences are computed on the
  same holdout split for comparison only. They are not an audit result and will
  not be used to reinterpret the audit.
- No threshold, feature, model, or mapping will be tuned after seeing results. If
  the result is "no material bias", it will be reported as that.
- For transparency: reduced-size plumbing runs (200 observational applicants, 40
  pairs, `min_pairs` 5, over ASGI) were used during R3a adapter development to
  verify skip events and twin feasibility. They are not the evaluation, use no
  frozen audit settings, and were not used to form any expectation above.
